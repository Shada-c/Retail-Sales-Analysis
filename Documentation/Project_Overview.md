# Retail Sales Analysis Project

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing retail sales data to understand sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance, payment patterns, and overall business performance.

The project uses six related datasets to perform an end-to-end retail sales analysis.

## 2. Project Objective

The main objective is to analyse retail sales data, identify meaningful business insights, understand customer purchasing patterns, evaluate product performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 3. Dataset Description

The project consists of six datasets:

1. **Orders:** Contains order-level information, including order ID, customer ID, order date, and order status.
2. **Order Details:** Contains product-level information for each order, including product ID, quantity, unit price, and sales details.
3. **Customers:** Contains customer information such as customer ID, name, gender, age, and location.
4. **Products:** Contains product information, including product ID, product name, category, brand, and pricing details.
5. **Stores:** Contains store information, including store ID, store name, city, state, and location details.
6. **Payments:** Contains payment information, including payment ID, order ID, payment method, payment amount, and payment status.

- Sales and revenue analysis
- Product and category performance
- Customer purchasing behaviour
- Payment method analysis

## 7. Project Outcome

An interactive Power BI dashboard presenting key business insights, sales performance, customer trends, product performance, and payment patterns to support better business decisions.
